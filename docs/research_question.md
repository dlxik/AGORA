# Research Question

## Working question

How can a traceable temporal argument graph represent changes in public claims and authoritative information without using hindsight?

## Supporting questions

1. How should premises, rules, conclusions, sources, and validity intervals be represented?
2. How should argument status and support or attack relations change across information states?
3. What source and annotation procedures provide sufficient auditability for manual case studies?
4. Can the representation transfer from the CTQ master case to a small household business taxation validation case?

These questions are working formulations and require supervisor review before being treated as final research questions.
