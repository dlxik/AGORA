Non-redundancy decisions:

- CTQ-C036 concerns the operative status of a proposal, unlike CTQ-C003's general competence rule or CTQ-C022's stakeholder-legitimacy claim.
- CTQ-C037 records an early scope challenge before the all-subject evidence existed; it does not backdate the t6 finding.
- CTQ-C038 separates application access from substantive admission eligibility, which is not covered by the later result-processing candidate.
- CTQ-C039 and CTQ-C040 add distinct measurement confounds: test-form difficulty and population-denominator consistency.
- CTQ-C041 grounds equal admission treatment in the original 2026 regulation, while CTQ-C043 records the later state change to usable replacement results.
- CTQ-C042 uses cross-subject heterogeneity rather than the earlier Mathematics-only distribution arguments.
- CTQ-C044 distinguishes diagnostic comparison from treating the invalid first sitting as ground truth.
- CTQ-C045 preserves alternative valid admission pathways without claiming that any candidate necessarily qualified through them.

Rejected or merged during batch 5:

- Additional reports repeating the provincial Math-only proposal or the final all-subject decision were rejected as duplicates.
- A second no-retest candidate was rejected because CTQ-C002 and CTQ-C005 already preserve that conclusion from expert and social viewpoints.
- Claims that the retest score decline proved cheating, low ability, or guilt for all candidates were rejected as overstrong.
- Claims that a particular test was definitively harder were weakened to a test-difficulty confound because no formal equating study was found.
- A separate candidate that the admissions workflow protected both CTQ candidates and third parties was merged conceptually with CTQ-C009 and CTQ-C041.
- The original Decision 2288 and Ministry-hosted Official Letter 5615 were not found; repeated references to them were not assigned new source IDs.
